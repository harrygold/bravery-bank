Bravery Bank — Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 1, 2026

The short version

Bravery Bank does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device. We no servers, no accounts, and no analytics.

What data the app stores

Bravery Bank saves your progress locally on your device using your phone's built-in storage. This includes your brave days count, which challenges you've completed, your notification preferences, and your app settings. This data never leaves your device.

What we do NOT collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, device identifiers, usage analytics, crash reports, or any other personal information. We have no user accounts and no login system.

Third-party services

Bravery Bank does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The app does not contain ads.

Notifications

If you choose to enable daily reminders, notifications are scheduled locally on your device. No data is sent to any server to deliver these notifications.

Data deletion

You can delete all your data at any time by using the "Reset All Data" option in the app's Settings screen. You can also uninstall the app, which removes all stored data from your device.

Children's privacy

Bravery Bank is designed for adults and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from children.

Changes to this policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will post the revised version at this URL with an updated date. Since the app has no accounts or contact information, we cannot notify users directly of changes.
